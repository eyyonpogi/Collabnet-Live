About CollabNet

CollabNet is the official digital bridge between Our Lady of Lourdes College Foundation (OLLCF) and the Local Government Unit (LGU) of Vinzons, Camarines Norte for community extension programs and civic partnerships.

What CollabNet Addresses

Traditional outreach coordination often suffers from delayed announcements, manual paper attendance sheets, unverified volunteer hours, and scattered Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) tracking. CollabNet centralizes activity proposals, enforces on-site PIN-verified Time-In/Time-Out logs, and keeps student extension records transparent and accountable.

Why Join as a Student Volunteer?
